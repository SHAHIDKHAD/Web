SERVER RULES

To keep our galaxy running smoothly, follow these rules and USE COMMON SENSE.

  • Treat everyone with respect – No hate, bullying, or drama.
  • Keep it clean – No NSFW or disturbing content.
  • No excessive pinging, spam, or promotions.
  • Listen to staff – Don't argue if you're warned.
  • VC Rules – No loud noises, echo mics, or trolling.

  • No hacks, cheats, or auto-clickers – Play fair.

  • No spawn camping or exploiting bugs.
