Voting Guide

Why Vote?

  • Keep the adventure alive and help SpaceMC grow! Your daily vote boosts our server visibility and brings more players to the community.

  • Vote for SpaceMC every single day to receive free in-game items such as Crate Keys and money.

  • You can vote once per day on each site to earn rewards.

How to Vote

  • Click on one of the vote links above.

  • Java Edition: Simply click "Vote" and join the server to claim your rewards.

  • Bedrock / Pocket Edition: Add a period (.) before your username (e.g. .Username), click "Vote", then join the server to claim your rewards.

  • Complete the CAPTCHA (if any) to finish voting.
